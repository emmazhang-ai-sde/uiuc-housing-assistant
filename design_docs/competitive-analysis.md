# Competitive Analysis — UIUC Housing Assistant

## Purpose

Understanding the existing landscape before building in public. This doc identifies who else is in this space, what they do well, and where the gap is that this project fills.

*Last updated: June 2026*

---

## Existing UIUC-Specific Platforms

---

### Student.com (UIUC page)
**URL:** student.com/us/champaign-urbana/u/university-of-illinois-urbana-champaign

Large global student housing aggregator with a UIUC section. Launched an AI-powered instant-booking platform in January 2025, but the AI is focused on booking speed, not natural language property discovery.

**Strengths:**
- Global brand, large inventory
- Live chat and WhatsApp support
- AI booking pipeline (fast turnaround for partners)

**Weaknesses:**
- Not UIUC-native — the UIUC page is one of thousands of university pages
- AI is for booking logistics, not "find me a 2BR near Grainger under $900"
- No student identity verification
- Commercial marketplace — listings favor paying partners

---

### uhomes.com
**URL:** en.uhomes.com/us/urbana-champaign

International student–focused housing aggregator with photos, videos, and virtual consultants.

**Strengths:**
- Photos and video walkthroughs
- Targets international students specifically (relevant for UIUC's large international population)

**Weaknesses:**
- No AI or NL search
- Not UIUC-verified
- Aggregator model — does not scrape local companies directly

---

### UIUC Official Housing
**URL:** housing.illinois.edu

University-run platform for on-campus dorms and university-managed housing only. Not relevant for off-campus apartment search.

---

## AI Housing Tools (Not UIUC-Specific)

### STAN.ai
**URL:** stan.ai/sector/student-housing

AI chatbot for the student housing industry — but designed for building *management*, not search. Helps existing residents book amenities, submit maintenance requests, and ask account questions.

**Not a competitor** — it serves landlords and property managers, not students searching for housing.

## Positioning for "Build in Public"

The unoccupied position is clear: **the only UIUC housing search tool built by a student, for students, with no ads, no broker fees, and plain English search.**

Suggested one-liner for social posts:
> *"I built an AI-powered housing search for UIUC students — ask in plain English, get ranked results. No ads, no paid listings, UIUC emails only."*

RentCollegePads is the strongest existing tool and worth referencing in posts — framing this project as "what happens when a CS student builds the version they actually wanted" is honest and compelling.
